- **[FYSA - Supply Chain / Government Oversight: Wyoming Food Freedom Act, 10+ years with no confirmed outbreaks](https://ij.org/press-release/new-data-no-foodborne-illness-reported-in-seven-states-that-allow-sale-of-homemade-perishable-food/)** _(Intel Briefing, Nov 11)_ — Since 2015, Wyoming has let residents sell homemade food (shelf-stable goods, dairy, eggs, raw milk, limited poultry, rabbit and fish) to informed in-state buyers without a license or inspection. The cap is 250,000 units or $250,000 a year, and the state Health Dept can still investigate illness. Institute for Justice FOIA data from seven states shows zero confirmed foodborne illness cases from these sales. US impact: the briefing says this supports expanding direct-to-consumer homemade food sales in other states, giving citizens more local food access and small-business opportunities. No documents attached.
