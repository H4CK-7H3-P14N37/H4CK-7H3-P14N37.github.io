Curse Catcher — Critical CVEs

VMware (Broadcom) vCenter

  • ■ CVE-2026-123890: Directory Service authentication bypass in vCenter (CVSS 9.8) (Published Nov 19) — A network attacker can skip authentication in the VMware Directory Service and get into vCenter. Fixed in 9.1.1.0200, 9.0.2.0200, 8.0 U3m and 5.2.5; Telco Cloud Platform and Infrastructure are also affected, and there is no workaround. CISA's rating shows no known exploitation and it isn't in KEV yet, but Mandiant/Google and press reports say the China-linked group Armory Castle (formerly UNC5221) has exploited it as a zero-day since September to gain admin access and steal data. Treat it as exploited: patch now and keep vCenter off untrusted networks. Source Broadcom advisory Google Threat Intelligence BleepingComputer The Hacker News The Hacker News (advisory).

JetBrains TeamCity

  • ■ CVE-2026-132891: remote code execution through external pull requests (CVSS 9.8) (Published Nov 19) — Fixed in 2026.2, 2026.1.4 and 2025.11.8. CISA rates it as scriptable at scale with full control, with no known exploitation; not in KEV. TeamCity has been mass-exploited by ransomware and state groups before, and this one is reachable by anyone who can open a pull request, so upgrade quickly and review settings that build external PRs. Source JetBrains fixed issues.

OpenStack Cinder

  • ■ CVE-2026-133189: volume owners can inject commands into storage array management (CVSS 9.6) (Published Nov 19) — In SSH-based storage drivers, a tenant who owns a volume can send crafted connection info that runs commands on the array with Cinder's backend account.
