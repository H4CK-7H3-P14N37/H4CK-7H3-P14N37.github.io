## Curse Catcher — Critical CVEs

### VMware (Broadcom) vCenter

- **[CVE-2026-123890: Directory Service authentication bypass in vCenter (CVSS 9.8)](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-123890)** _(Published Nov 19)_ — A network attacker can skip authentication in the VMware Directory Service and get into vCenter. Fixed in 9.1.1.0200, 9.0.2.0200, 8.0 U3m and 5.2.5; Telco Cloud Platform and Infrastructure are also affected, and there is no workaround. CISA's rating shows no known exploitation and it isn't in KEV yet, but Mandiant/Google and press reports say the China-linked group Armory Castle (formerly UNC5221) has exploited it as a zero-day since September to gain admin access and steal data. Treat it as exploited: patch now and keep vCenter off untrusted networks.  
  [Broadcom advisory](https://support.broadcom.com/web/ecx/support-content-notification/-/external/content/SecurityAdvisories/0/39495) · [Google Threat Intelligence](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/armory-castle-exploits-vsphere/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/vmware-patches-vcenter-zero-day-exploited-in-attacks-since-september/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/china-linked-hackers-exploit-critical.html) · [The Hacker News (advisory)](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/critical-vmware-vcenter-flaw-cvss-98.html)

### OpenStack Cinder

- **[CVE-2026-133189: volume owners can inject commands into storage array management (CVSS 9.6)](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-133189)** _(Published Nov 19)_ — In SSH-based storage drivers, a tenant who owns a volume can send crafted connection info that runs commands on the array with Cinder's backend account.
